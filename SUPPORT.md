# Support

Use the repository's issue forms for reproducible corpus defects and fixture
proposals. Use a discussion or design issue for broader methodology questions.

Before reporting a problem:

1. pin the full corpus commit;
2. name the case ID and expected criterion or outcome;
3. record the browser, runtime, viewport, preferences and interaction state;
4. distinguish a rule result from capture, hydration, sampling or state-reach
   failure;
5. remove credentials, personal data, production captures and private paths.

Product-specific support, legal conformance decisions and remediation services
are outside the scope of this repository. A corpus result is benchmark evidence,
not certification of a website, application, document or product.

Security, privacy and unsafe-media reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
