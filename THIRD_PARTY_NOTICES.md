# Third-party sources and attribution

The corpus uses or derives structured reference data from the following public
standards sources. Normative text remains at the source; local data is limited
to the fields needed for traceability and repeatable testing.

## World Wide Web Consortium

- [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)
- [Accessibility Conformance Testing Rules](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/)
- [WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Guide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/)
- [Website Accessibility Conformance Evaluation Methodology](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG-EM/)

These materials are identified in `FOUNDATION.md` as covered by the
[W3C Software and Document License](https://www.w3.org/copyright/software-license-2023/).
The repository preserves source URLs and does not represent derived summaries
as normative W3C text or W3C endorsement.

Individual tool and platform names may appear for interoperability and research
purposes. Their owners do not sponsor or endorse this corpus unless explicitly
stated by them.
