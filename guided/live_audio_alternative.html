Is there a live equivalent for the live audio-only content?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 1.2.9 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

The alternative has to exist while the broadcast is happening and keep pace with it. A corpus can hold the question but not a live stream, and whether the equivalent keeps up is judged by someone following both at once.

When this does not apply

This criterion is inapplicable inapplicable when no live audio-only content is presented. Recording inapplicable is a correct result here, not a gap.

How to check it

  1. Join the live audio and the alternative at the same time.
  2. Check the alternative presents equivalent information, not a delayed summary.
  3. Check the lag stays small enough to follow the broadcast.
  4. Record who produced the alternative and how it is announced to the audience.

Evidence to capture

Recording of both streams with timestamps, and the production arrangement.
