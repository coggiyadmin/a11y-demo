Is pronunciation available where meaning is ambiguous without it?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 3.1.6 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

The criterion applies only where the word cannot be understood without knowing how it is said. Deciding that a heteronym is genuinely ambiguous in its context, rather than resolved by the surrounding sentence, is a reading judgement.

When this does not apply

This criterion is inapplicable inapplicable where no word is ambiguous without its pronunciation. Recording inapplicable is a correct result here, not a gap.

How to check it

  1. Find words whose meaning changes with pronunciation in this context.
  2. Check whether the sentence already disambiguates them; if it does, the criterion does not apply.
  3. For the rest, check a pronunciation is given inline, in a glossary, or as ruby annotation.
  4. Listen with a screen reader to confirm the mechanism survives spoken output.

Evidence to capture

The ambiguous words, their context, and the pronunciation mechanism used.
