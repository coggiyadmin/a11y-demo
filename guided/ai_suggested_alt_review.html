Is the AI-suggested text alternative correct for THIS context?

Method: ai-assisted · Criteria: 1.1.1 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

A model can describe what is in an image. It cannot know why the image is on the page, which is what the alternative has to convey — the same photo needs different alt text in a news story and a checkout page. Suggestions are a starting point and must be verified; shipping them unreviewed replaces a missing alt with a confidently wrong one, which is harder to find.

How to check it

  1. Read the suggestion against the surrounding content, not the image alone.
  2. Ask what the reader would lose if the image vanished, and check the text supplies it.
  3. Reject descriptions of appearance where purpose is what matters.
  4. Mark decorative images empty rather than accepting a description.

Evidence to capture

The suggestion, the accepted text, and who approved it.
