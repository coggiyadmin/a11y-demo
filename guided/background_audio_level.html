Is the background at least 20 dB below the foreground narration?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 1.4.7 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

The separation is measurable, but only from the rendered audio, and deciding which passages are foreground narration rather than incidental sound is a listening judgement. Markup says nothing about either.

When this does not apply

This criterion is inapplicable inapplicable to musical performance and where spoken content is incidental. Recording inapplicable is a correct result here, not a gap.

How to check it

  1. Identify the passages that are foreground narration.
  2. Measure the level of the narration and of the background beneath it.
  3. Allow the exception for two or fewer seconds of incidental sound.
  4. If a control mutes the background entirely, record that instead of a measurement.

Evidence to capture

Level measurements per passage, the tool used, and the audio file.
