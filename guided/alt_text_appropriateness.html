Does each text alternative serve the same purpose as the image?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 1.1.1 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

A tool can detect a missing or empty alt. It cannot tell whether "image1.jpg" or "a photo" conveys what the image is there to convey — the commonest real failure is alt text that is present and useless.

How to check it

  1. Read the page with images replaced by their alt text.
  2. Ask whether the page still makes sense and nothing is lost.
  3. Check decorative images are empty-alt, not described.
  4. Check complex images have a long description or data equivalent.

Evidence to capture

The page with images disabled, and the alt text listed.

Fixture to check against

static/img_no_alt.html
