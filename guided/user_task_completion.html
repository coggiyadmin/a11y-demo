Can people with disabilities actually complete this task?

Method: user-testing · Criteria: beyond conformance · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

Conformance is not usability. A page can meet every criterion and still be unusable, and the only way to find that out is to watch people try. No corpus, and no tool, substitutes for this.

How to check it

  1. Recruit participants who use the relevant assistive technology daily.
  2. Set a real task, not a tour of the interface.
  3. Measure completion and time, and record where people give up.
  4. Pay participants for their expertise.

Evidence to capture

Session recordings with consent, completion rates, and quotes.
