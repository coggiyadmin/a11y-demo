Is a definition available for every word used in an unusual or restricted way?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 3.1.3 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

Whether a word is unusual depends on who is reading. Jargon, idiom and words given a narrow technical meaning all qualify, and none of them is detectable by looking the word up — the same word is ordinary in one audience and opaque in another.

How to check it

  1. Decide who the content is for before judging any word.
  2. List terms of art, idioms, and ordinary words given a narrow meaning here.
  3. Check each has a definition inline, in a glossary, or in a linked dictionary entry.
  4. Check the mechanism is reachable from the place the word is used.

Evidence to capture

The term list, the audience assumption, and where each definition was found.
