Is the content understandable without specialist knowledge?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 3.1.5 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

Readability formulas measure sentence and word length, not comprehensibility. A short sentence of jargon scores well and communicates nothing.

How to check it

  1. Identify the task the reader must complete.
  2. Check terms of art are explained at first use.
  3. Check a summary exists for anything long or procedural.

Evidence to capture

The passage, plus a note on who was asked and what they understood.
