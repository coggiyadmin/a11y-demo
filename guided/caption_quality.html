Do the captions convey the same information as the audio?

Method: guided-manual · Criteria: 1.2.2 · Automated outcome: cannot-tell

Why automation cannot decide this

A tool can confirm a caption track exists and parses. It cannot judge accuracy, synchronisation, or whether speakers are identified.

How to check it

  1. Play the media with sound off and captions on.
  2. Check every speaker is identified when more than one person speaks.
  3. Check meaningful sound effects are described, not just dialogue.
  4. Check captions stay in sync and are readable at playback speed.

Evidence to capture

Recording of playback with captions on, plus the caption file.
