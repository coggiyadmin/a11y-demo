# a11y-benchmark v0.1.0

The first immutable public baseline contains 226 public synthetic cases across
11 fixture families. It publishes product-neutral corpus and result contracts
for repeatable accessibility-tool evaluation.

## Included

- 113 declared positive cases, 104 corrected/negative controls and 9 guided
  checks;
- isolated rule fixtures, stateful interactions, multi-page journeys, delivery
  and capture cases, component patterns, media, flash-safety, colour-independent
  information, UI states and non-web proxy/native surface experiments;
- corpus manifest format 1.0.0 and benchmark-result format 1.0.0;
- stable family digests, global case-ID validation and fixture-path validation;
- public contribution, security, privacy, attribution and result-publication
  policies; and
- Apache-2.0 licensing for original project material with source-specific W3C
  terms preserved.

## Known gaps

- Four documented pipeline-integrity cases remain planned: `deep_site`,
  `entry_not_sampled`, `vacuous_pass` and `no_declared_flow`.
- Twenty WCAG criteria without ACT or local ground truth are all AAA and still
  require method classification or fixtures.
- Seven of eighteen surfaces are represented. Native iOS, Android, desktop,
  PDF, office-document, kiosk, hardware, immersive and support-environment lanes
  remain incomplete.
- Representation does not imply execution or independent verification.
- Guided manual review, assistive-technology interoperability and user-task
  evaluation require people and remain separate from automated metrics.

The tracked roadmap is in issues #17 through #25.

## Use and citation

Pin tag `v0.1.0` or its full commit SHA and record the SHA-256 of
`corpus-manifest.json`. Validate published evidence against the attached schema
and follow `PUBLISHING_RESULTS.md`.

This release is not WCAG certification, legal advice, product endorsement or a
guarantee about a production website. Errors, unsupported cases,
`cannot-tell`, unobserved targets and zero-examined executions are not passes.
